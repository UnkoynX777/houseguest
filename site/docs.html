How a session works

  1. You pick a profile (a set of allow and block rules), one or more guest accounts and a duration.
  2. Houseguest compiles and analyses the policy, flags conflicts, and shows a preview.
  3. The service observes process creation under the guest account and terminates anything the rules do not allow.
  4. At the deadline the service lifts the restrictions and, if you chose, disconnects or signs the guest out.
